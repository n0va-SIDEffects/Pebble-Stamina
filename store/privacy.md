# Stamina – Privacy Policy

*Last updated: September 2026*

Stamina is designed so that your data stays on your watch.

**What is stored**
Session data (duration, movement counts, rhythm, heart rate, estimated calories, sleep and activity summaries, mood ratings, learned positions, position names and partner initials) and your settings (sex, age, weight and app options). Everything is stored only in the app's local storage on your Pebble watch.

**What is shared**
By default nothing. Stamina has no account, no own server, no analytics and no advertising.

**Optional: timeline pins**
If you turn on "Timeline pins" (off by default), the Pebble app on your phone sends a short pin to the Rebble timeline server so it appears in your watch timeline: for a session only the date, duration and estimated kcal; for an achievement its name and description. Nothing else is sent. Turn the option off to stop this.

**Phone connection**
The settings page in the Pebble app runs on your phone. It keeps a copy of your settings (not your session data) in the phone app's local storage so the page can show your current values. It is not transmitted anywhere else.

**Voice input**
If you name a position by voice, the watch uses the Pebble dictation service provided by the Pebble app. Only the spoken name is processed for transcription; Stamina itself does not record or keep audio.

**Health data**
Stamina reads heart rate, sleep and step data from Pebble Health on your watch to compute its statistics. It does not write to or export Pebble Health data.

**Deleting your data**
Settings > Delete all data removes all sessions. Uninstalling the app removes all stored data from the watch.
